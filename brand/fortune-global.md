# Fortune Global Organisation: brand notes

Source: https://fortuneglobalorg.com (read 2026-11-18).

## Who they are
- An outsourced sales and marketing company based in Hyderabad, operating **since 2010**.
- Takes clients' products directly to consumers through dedicated teams, "combining the information of advertising with the reassurance of word-of-mouth."
- Its core interest is people: award-winning mentoring that turns fresh graduates and post-graduates into **independent entrepreneurs**.
- Presence across **Telangana and Andhra Pradesh**, with headquarters in Begumpet, Hyderabad.
- Client sectors: banking, insurance, entertainment, internet, IT, social media, global technology. Partners named on the site: HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Kotak Mahindra, Yes Bank, IndusInd Bank, AU Small Finance Bank, Sumitomo Mitsui Financial, Generali Central Insurance, Sony LIV.

## Visual identity
- **Logo:** three arcs (blue on top, red-orange on the left, green on the right) around a white centre, with "FORTUNE / GLOBAL ORGANISATION" in black below. The file is `brand/logo.png`, also hosted at `https://fortuneglobalorg.com/assets/logo-EbbXP8rC.png`.
- **Site colors:** deep navy `#1A2541` (primary) and a warm orange-gold accent (CSS `oklch(72% .21 42)`), on an off-white background.
- **Video look:** navy shadows, gold highlights, confident young professionals in business suits, award-stage energy.

## Voice
Ambitious, warm and mentoring: "we believe in you" rather than hype. Short, punchy lines, with an aspiration-to-achievement arc.
